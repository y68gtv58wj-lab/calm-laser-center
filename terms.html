5. After your treatment

Temporary skin reactions are normal after laser hair removal. They can include redness, warmth and slight swelling similar to a mild sunburn, small bumps around the hair follicles, and skin that feels rough, sensitive or tender for a few days. These reactions usually fade within a few hours to a few days. Treated hairs shed over the following days to weeks.

Less commonly, blistering, crusting or temporary darkening or lightening of the skin can occur; in rare cases, changes in skin color or texture can be lasting. Following the aftercare instructions – especially sun protection with SPF 30 or higher and avoiding tanning – helps reduce these risks.

Please contact us right away at (818) 808-0010 if you notice blisters, crusting, pain that gets worse after 48 hours, signs of infection, or redness that lasts longer than three days.
