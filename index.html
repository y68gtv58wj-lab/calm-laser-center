Our laser

Candela GentleLase Pro-U

Your treatment is performed with the GentleLase Pro-U by Candela – a professional alexandrite laser system for laser hair removal.

  • 755 nm alexandrite wavelength

    Targets the pigment in the hair follicle. Candela recommends it for hair removal on skin types I–IV.

  • Dynamic Cooling Device (DCD)

    A brief burst of cooling spray protects the skin a split second before each laser pulse, for a more comfortable treatment.

  • Spot sizes from 6 to 18 mm

    Small spots for precise areas like the upper lip, larger spots to cover legs and back quickly.

    It's normal for the treated skin to look red, feel warm or slightly swollen – similar to a mild sunburn – and for small bumps to appear around the hair follicles. The skin can also feel rough or tender for a few days. These reactions usually fade within a few hours to a few days. Treated hairs don't fall out right away: they shed over the following days to weeks, which can look like regrowth at first.

    Use a cool compress if the area feels warm, keep the skin moisturized and protect it from the sun with SPF 30 or higher.
