1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Online booking: first and last name, email address, phone number, the treatments you select, your appointment date and time, and any note you add.
  • When you contact us by phone, text message, email or Instagram: the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: when you load the Website, your browser connects to our hosting provider and to the third-party services listed in section 3. These providers receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the page requested, which they may log for security and operation.
  • No tracking by us: we do not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or marketing cookies on the Website. The Website does not set its own cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • to schedule, confirm, change and manage your appointments;
  • to send appointment confirmations and reminders by email or text message;
  • to answer your questions and communicate with you about your visit;
  • to keep business records as required by law (for example, for tax purposes);
  • to keep the Website and booking system secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How long we keep your information

We keep booking and client records for as long as you are a client and afterwards for as long as needed for our legitimate business purposes and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate;
  • delete your personal information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop sending you reminders by email or text message.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, including California privacy law. To make a request, email us at calmlaseraz@gmail.com or call (818) 808-0010. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

Text messages: you can reply STOP to any appointment text message from our booking system to stop receiving texts, where this option is offered, or contact us directly.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” setting. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Website does not change its behavior in response to these signals.

7. Security

The Website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and booking requests are sent over encrypted connections to our booking provider. Access to client records is limited to our staff. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 online. Appointments for minors must be made by a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Links to other websites

The Website links to other sites, such as Instagram, Google Maps and Square. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact Calm Laser Center Corporation at calmlaseraz@gmail.com, by phone at (818) 808-0010, or by mail at 4419 Van Nuys Blvd, Ste 411, Sherman Oaks, CA 91403.
